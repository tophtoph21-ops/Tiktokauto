Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AutoContent Studio, available at https://tiktokauto-production.up.railway.app, processes information when you use the service.

1. Information we process

Depending on the features you use, the service may process account configuration, app settings, product information, generated content, scheduling information, performance metrics and technical logs.

2. TikTok data

If you connect TikTok, the service may process information made available through the permissions you authorize, such as basic account identifiers, your own video information, publishing status and video performance metrics including views, likes, comments and shares.

3. TikTok credentials and tokens

OAuth access and refresh tokens may be stored so that the service can perform the functions you authorize. Stored secrets are protected using application-level encryption. The service does not request or store your TikTok password.

4. AI service data

When you request AI-generated text, audio, images or related content, relevant prompts and content may be sent to the configured AI provider in order to provide the requested feature.

5. How information is used

Information is used to operate the service, generate and render content, manage schedules, publish authorized content, synchronize metrics, improve automation decisions, troubleshoot errors and maintain security.

6. Sharing

Information may be transmitted to third-party providers only as necessary to provide requested features, such as hosting infrastructure, AI providers and TikTok APIs. We do not sell personal information.

7. Data retention

Data is retained for as long as needed to operate the service or until it is deleted by the operator. OAuth access can be revoked by disconnecting the TikTok account or through the relevant TikTok account settings.

8. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect stored data. However, no Internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices

You can stop using the service, disconnect connected accounts and revoke third-party permissions. You may also request deletion of locally stored service data from the operator of the application.

10. Children's privacy

The service is not intended for children below the minimum age required to use the connected platforms and services.

11. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to the service, third-party APIs or legal requirements. The latest version will be available on this page.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, use the service website at https://tiktokauto-production.up.railway.app.
